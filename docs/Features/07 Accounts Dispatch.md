# 07 · Accounts Dispatch

Senior Producer pushes approved work to accounts, in labelled batches.

## Triggers
- **25th monthly** auto-reminder to SrProd + all producers (§7 #10).
- **Mid-month** manual early batch for urgent vendor releases; also pings producers to wrap up (§7 #11).
- **Month-end** final dispatch, manually triggered once all approved.

## Contents of a dispatch
Approved closing sheets + their invoices + reimbursement summary PDF. Labelled **"Mid-Month Batch"** or
**"Month-End Batch"**. Sent to accounts (§7 #12).

## Audit
Full dispatch log: who, when, what included (`Dispatch.includedSheetIds`). Any sheet still pending at month-end →
escalation to SrProd (§7 #14).
